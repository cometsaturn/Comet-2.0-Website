Meet Comet 2.0

A fun-focused Discord bot built for communities.

About Comet

Comet 2.0 is a private, fun-focused Discord bot designed to provide games, entertainment and lightweight community features.

Comet uses Discord slash commands and interactive components such as buttons and select menus. Traditional message-prefix commands are not supported.

Comet uses Discord's official bot API and only requests the functionality required for its current features.

Comet does not intentionally scrape or archive users' general Discord conversations.

Comet 2.0 was developed with help from AI code.

Play interactive games with other members.
