Stockfish

Comet 2.0's chess functionality uses Stockfish , a free and open-source chess engine used to provide computer-opponent functionality.

Stockfish is an independent third-party project. It remains subject to the GNU General Public License version 3.0.

Local software usage

Stockfish is intended to be installed and operated locally on the Raspberry Pi hosting Comet 2.0. It is used by the chess command to provide computer-opponent functionality.

The Stockfish executable is not distributed to Discord users. Users interact with the chess functionality through the Discord bot without receiving the engine itself.
